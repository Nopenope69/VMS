# Chief Product Officer (CPO) Reference & Playbook

## 1. Product-Market Fit (PMF) Quantification
- **Sean Ellis PMF Survey:** Ask active users: *"How would you feel if you could no longer use [Product]?"*
  - **>= 40% "Very Disappointed":** Strong statistical correlation with organic product-market fit.
  - **< 40%:** Do not ramp paid growth; focus exclusively on customer retention and problem validation.
- **Retention Curve Flattening:** In cohort analysis, the percentage of retained users must plateau asymptotically. If the curve continuously slopes down toward zero, you have leaky retention.

---

## 2. Roadmap Prioritization Frameworks
### RICE Framework
$$\text{Score} = \frac{\text{Reach} \times \text{Impact} \times \text{Confidence}}{\text{Effort}}$$
- **Reach:** Customers affected per time period.
- **Impact:** 3 (massive), 2 (high), 1 (medium), 0.5 (low).
- **Confidence:** 100% (high data), 80% (some data), 50% (gut feel).
- **Effort:** Person-months required.

---

## 3. Discovery vs. Delivery Discipline
- **Continuous Discovery (Teresa Torres):** Conduct weekly customer interviews to test assumptions before writing production code.
- **Riskiest Assumption Testing (RAT):** Instead of building the full feature (MVP), build the smallest possible test to validate the underlying user assumption.
