# Board Communications Director Reference & Playbook

## 1. Board Meeting Preparation & The 48-Hour Pre-Read
- **Pre-Read Delivery:** Distribute board materials at least 48 to 72 hours before the meeting. A board meeting where the CEO reads slides aloud is an operational failure.
- **Structure of the Board Deck:**
  1. **Executive Summary & State of the Union:** 2 slides on performance, cash runway, and major wins/misses.
  2. **Key Financials & Dashboards:** P&L, ARR waterfall, burn rate, cash balance.
  3. **Strategic Discussion Topics (2-3 items):** Deep dive on major pivots, M&A, hiring, or expansion.
  4. **Governance & Approvals:** Option grants, budget approvals, legal authorizations.
  5. **Appendix:** Detailed departmental metrics.

---

## 2. The Golden Rule: No Surprises in the Boardroom
- If there is a major revenue miss, key executive departure, or litigation threat, **never** present it for the first time during the formal board meeting.
- **Pre-Wiring:** Call lead directors individually 48-72 hours in advance. Walk them through what happened, your root-cause analysis, and your proposed remediation plan. Secure their alignment in private so the boardroom remains focused on constructive solutions.
