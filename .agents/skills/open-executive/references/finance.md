# Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Reference & Playbook

## 1. Core Unit Economics Formulas & Thresholds

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) & Payback
$$\text{CAC} = \frac{\text{Total Sales & Marketing Spend (Fully Burdened)}}{\text{Number of New Customers Acquired}}$$

$$\text{CAC Payback Period (Months)} = \frac{\text{CAC}}{\text{Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)} \times \text{Gross Margin (\%)}}$$
- **Healthy Threshold:**
  - SMB: < 12 months
  - Mid-Market: 12 - 15 months
  - Enterprise: 15 - 18 months max

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
$$\text{LTV} = \frac{\text{ARPU} \times \text{Gross Margin (\%)}}{\text{Monthly Churn Rate}}$$

$$\text{LTV : CAC Ratio} \ge 3:1$$
- $\le 1.5:1$: Unsustainable burn; unit model broken.
- $3:1 - 5:1$: Golden zone of capital efficiency.
- $> 6:1$: Under-investing in growth; competitors will capture the market.

## 2. Capital Efficiency & Efficiency Metrics

### Burn Multiple
$$\text{Burn Multiple} = \frac{\text{Net Burn}}{\text{Net New ARR}}$$
- **< 1.0x:** Best-in-class efficiency.
- **1.0x - 1.5x:** Good venture performance.
- **1.5x - 2.0x:** Suspect; examine CAC and churn.
- **> 2.0x:** Crisis mode; cutting burn required immediately.

### Rule of 40
$$\text{YoY Revenue Growth Rate (\%)} + \text{Free Cash Flow Margin (\%)} \ge 40\%$$
- Standard health test for growth-stage and mature SaaS businesses.

### Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
$$\text{NRR} = \frac{\text{Beginning ARR} + \text{Expansions} - \text{Contractions} - \text{Churn}}{\text{Beginning ARR}} \times 100$$
- **< 90%:** Leaky bucket; unsustainable without massive acquisition spend.
- **100% - 110%:** Solid SMB performance.
- **> 120%:** Elite enterprise benchmark (negative net churn).

## 3. Cash Management & Runway Operating Rules
1. **The 12-Month Rule:** Never allow cash reserves to dip below 12 months of net burn without an active, signed financing term sheet or an executable contingency plan.
2. **Default Alive vs. Default Dead (Paul Graham):**
   - *Default Alive:* Current cash + reasonable growth leads to profitability before running out of money.
   - *Default Dead:* Reaching profitability requires raising another round at an optimistic valuation. Always know which state you are in.
3. **Fundraising Timing:** Initiate investor conversations 6 to 9 months before cash reaches zero. Never raise with less than 4 months of runway; desperate founders accept punitive liquidation preferences.
