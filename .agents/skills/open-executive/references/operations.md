# Chief Operating Officer (COO) Reference & Playbook

## 1. Theory of Constraints (Goldratt)
1. **Identify the Constraint:** Find the single process step that determines the maximum throughput of the entire company (e.g., sales engineering onboarding, customer data migration).
2. **Exploit the Constraint:** Ensure the bottleneck operates at 100% capacity with zero idle time or low-value distractions.
3. **Subordinate Everything Else:** Align all upstream and downstream processes to support the bottleneck pace.
4. **Elevate the Constraint:** Invest capital or automation to permanently expand the constraint's throughput.
5. **Repeat:** Once the constraint is resolved, identify the new bottleneck.

---

## 2. Process Before Headcount
- **The Scalability Trap:** Hiring more people into an undocumented, erratic process simply multiplies errors and overhead.
- **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs):** Before adding headcount, run the process manually 10 times, document the SOP, automate repetitive steps with software, and hire only when human judgment is the bottleneck.

---

## 3. Vendor Management & SLA Enforcement
- **Tier 1 (Mission-Critical):** Core cloud hosting, primary database, payment processors. Must have contractual 99.95%+ uptime SLAs, dedicated account escalation paths, and tested disaster-recovery failover plans.
- **Single-Source Risk:** Always map dependencies. Never rely on a proprietary single-source vendor without an articulated migration path.
