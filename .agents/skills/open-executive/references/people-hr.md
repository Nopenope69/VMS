# Chief HR / People Officer (CHRO) Reference & Playbook

## 1. Organizational Design & Span of Control
- **Optimal Span:** 5 to 8 direct reports per executive or manager.
- **Fewer than 4:** Leads to micromanagement and excessive layers that distort executive communication.
- **More than 10:** Causes managerial bottlenecking, delayed feedback, and unaddressed underperformance.
- **Layer Count Rule:** A 100-person company rarely needs more than 3 layers (CEO -> VP/Director -> IC). Resist title inflation that creates ghost tiers.

---

## 2. Executive Hiring Framework
When evaluating senior leaders, evaluate against **Year-One Outcomes**, not pedigree:

1. **The 3-Month / 6-Month / 12-Month Mandate:**
   - What must be true 90 days in? (Team audit, quick wins, strategic roadmap).
   - What must be true 12 months in? (P&L impact, pipeline generated, platform delivered).
2. **Pedigree Trap:** Do not hire an executive whose success occurred only at 100x your scale with armies of coordinators. Early-stage executives must personally build the machine before managing it.
3. **Reference Checks:** Never rely solely on candidate-provided references. Conduct backdoor references with former direct reports and peers to identify how the candidate handles high-stress failures.

---

## 3. Compensation & Leveling Philosophy
- **Market Anchoring:** Target 50th percentile cash and 75th percentile equity for early-stage growth companies. Shift toward 75th percentile cash as the business reaches cashflow stability.
- **Standardized Bands:** Manage compensation by transparent role bands, not negotiated exceptions. One-off deals leak and destroy company-wide morale.
- **Equity Cliff & Refreshers:** Standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff. Implement performance-based equity refresh grants at year 2.5 to eliminate the departure cliff at year 4.

---

## 4. Performance Management & PIPs
- **Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs):**
  - Duration: 30, 60, or 90 days maximum.
  - Structure: Binary, objective metrics (e.g., "Close $150k in new ARR", "Ship feature X with zero Sev-1 bugs").
  - Purpose: A real final opportunity to succeed, not a bureaucratic termination ritual.
- **Action Speed:** Fast to hire, faster to part ways on values or behavioral toxicity. A brilliant jerk creates toxic drag that repels top-tier talent.
