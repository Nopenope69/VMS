# General Counsel (GC) Reference & Playbook

## 1. Commercial Contract Guardrails
Always review commercial contracts against these core terms:

1. **Limitation of Liability (LoL):**
   - Standard: Cap aggregate liability to the total fees paid by customer in the preceding 12 months.
   - Super-caps (2x - 3x fees): Reserve strictly for verifiable breaches of confidentiality or gross negligence.
   - Uncapped Liability: Never agree to uncapped liability for general breach of contract.
2. **Indemnification:**
   - Insist on mutual indemnification.
   - Company indemnifies for third-party IP infringement.
   - Customer indemnifies for customer data, illegal conduct, and unauthorized use of platform.
3. **IP Ownership:**
   - The company retains all right, title, and interest in and to the platform, underlying models, software, and aggregate metrics.
   - Customer owns their proprietary customer data.
   - Never allow a customer contract to assign rights to any "work product" or custom configurations developed during implementation.
4. **Payment & Term:**
   - Net 30 standard.
   - Remove automatic multi-year renewals that require unreasonable advance cancellation notice (e.g., 90 days).

---

## 2. IP & Clean Foundation
- **Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement (PIIA):**
  - Must be signed on or before Day 1 by every employee, contractor, and advisor.
  - Enforces automatic assignment of all inventions, code, and documentation created during engagement.
- **Open Source Software (OSS) Compliance:**
  - Strictly prohibit viral copyleft licenses (GPL, AGPL) in proprietary production codebases.
  - Maintain permissive licenses (Apache 2.0, MIT, BSD) for core infrastructure.

---

## 3. Worker Classification (Employee vs. 1099 Contractor)
- Factors creating employee status: behavioral control (dictating hours, tools, methods), financial control (furnishing equipment, expense reimbursement), relationship exclusivity.
- Misclassification risk: Retroactive payroll taxes, benefits liabilities, and statutory penalties.
