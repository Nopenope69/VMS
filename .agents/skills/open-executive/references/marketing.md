# Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) Reference & Playbook

## 1. Positioning & Category Design (April Dunford)
Positioning is not messaging; it is context setting. Strong positioning answers:
1. **Competitive Alternatives:** What would the customer do if you did not exist? (Excel, manual labor, legacy software).
2. **Unique Capabilities:** What can you deliver that the alternatives cannot?
3. **Value Proof:** What business outcome do those capabilities unlock? (Save 40 hours/month, reduce churn by 15%).
4. **Best-Fit Target Customer (ICP):** Who cares the most about this specific value?
5. **Market Category:** What frame of reference makes your value obvious?

---

## 2. GTM Sequencing: Phased Launch Strategy
1. **Alpha Phase:** Internal testing + 2-3 friendly users. Focus on baseline stability.
2. **Design Partner Phase:** 3 to 5 paying customers who get high-touch access in exchange for weekly feedback, co-designing workflows, and public case studies upon launch.
3. **Beta Phase:** Gated rollout to 25-50 users to test onboarding self-serve velocity and unassisted conversion.
4. **General Availability (GA):** Scaled launch with validated sales collateral, customer ROI proof points, and outbound distribution.

---

## 3. Funnel Economics & Channel Selection
- Measure drop-offs stage-by-stage: Visitor -> Lead -> MQL -> SQL -> Opportunity -> Closed Won.
- Focus on 1-2 primary acquisition channels until reaching $5M ARR (e.g., founder outbound + developer community). Multi-channel diversification before PMF dilutes focus.
